THE SOLUTION

A Digital Passport for
Every Public Asset.

Sanchay sits as an intelligence layer above existing government systems. It registers every government vehicle and public space as a Digital Asset Passport — with real-time GPS tracking, AI-powered idle detection, Airbnb-style civic booking, and a SHA-256 tamper-proof audit trail. No rip-and-replace. Pure plug-and-play.

Register

Every asset gets a Digital Passport with photos, QR code, GPS link, and compliance records. One-time setup per asset.

Track & Detect

Live GPS for vehicles. Occupancy sensors for spaces. AI flags idle assets — vehicles (6 months), spaces (3 days).

Book & Reuse

Citizens book idle civic spaces via calendar. Idle vehicles get re-use suggestions, inter-department transfers, or GeM auction flags.

THE PILOT

One City. Real Assets. Real Impact.

Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh — India's first city-wide government asset intelligence pilot.

  • 25 vehicles in Phase 1: 10 ambulances, 8 GMC trucks, 5 patrol, 2 PWD.
  • 10 spaces listed: 5 hospitals, 3 community halls, 2 classrooms.
  • 5 departments onboarded: Health, GMC, Police, PWD, Election.
  • 3-month MVP timeline: January to March 2026.
